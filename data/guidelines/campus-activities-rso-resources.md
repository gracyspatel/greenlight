## Space Requests

Recognized Student Organizations in good standing can request to book space on campus. This includes space in the Ronald Tutor Campus Center, outdoor venues, or classrooms so long as the space is available. Please be sure to adhere to the policies regarding social events as outlined in the Student Handbook. To submit a request form to book a space, please submit an online reservation request on the Trojan Event Services website. Questions about spaces managed by Trojan Event Services can be directed to scevents@usc.edu.

Groups interested in requesting a classroom space should submit the Classroom Scheduling form. Classroom Scheduling accepts requests for classroom spaces starting Week 4.

## Sales and Fundraising by RSOs

RSOs may not operate as businesses or generate income for individuals and/or businesses. RSOs or (individual members acting in any capacity for the RSO) may not conduct for-profit or commercial activities; conduct a business; act as a liaison, representative, agent, face or front for another business, person, or entity; or facilitate any of the activities listed above for others.

RSOs may fundraise to benefit their student organization, another RSO, or an outside philanthropic agency or relief effort, however organizations that solely exist to support an outside philanthropic organization will not be recognized. No individual student or person(s) may receive any type of benefit or payment in connection with any of these activities, either in the form of money, trade, discounts, and/or any other goods or services.

RSOs must submit a USC Events Permit Application (UEPA) for any events involving fundraising and meet with Campus Activities at least two weeks prior for fundraising approval. More information on RSO sales and fundraising can be found in the USC Student Handbook.

## USC Trojans Care for Trojans (TC4T)

USC Trojans Care for Trojans (TC4T) is an initiative within the Office of Campus Wellbeing and Crisis Intervention that empowers USC students, faculty and staff to take action when they are concerned about a fellow Trojan challenged with personal difficulties. This private and anonymous request form provides an opportunity for Trojans to help a member of our Trojan Family.

## Report & Response

USC Report & Response is your front door to reporting concerns and getting the support you need. Report as a Trojan or anonymously. Visit the USC Report & Response website for reporting options and resources.

## DPS Party Notification Form

In partnership with USC Student Life and DPS, the DPS Party Notification Form has been developed for all recognized student organizations and is an opportunity to notify DPS about your upcoming party.

The success of the DPS Party Notification Form relies upon the continued cooperation of students, faculty, staff, advisors, and alumni to understand the spirit of personal responsibility with regards to hosting an off-campus party.

As a reminder, use of the form does relieve any individual of their responsibilities toward those who attend your event or towards your neighbors.

USC wants you to be a good host and even better neighbor!
