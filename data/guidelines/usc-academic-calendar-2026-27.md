Official USC Registrar (Academic Records and Registrar) calendar. Fetched from https://arr.usc.edu/academic-calendar, which redirects here. Only the Fall 2026 and Spring 2027 sections are reproduced. The page does not list Summer 2027 or Fall 2027 dates.

# Fall 2026

## March
- March 2: Registration appointment times will be available for all currently enrolled students to review on Experience USC and Web Registration.

## August
- August 17-21: Registration for fall semester continues.
- August 21: Last day to register and settle without a late fee for session 001.
- August 24: Fall semester classes begin.

## September
- September 7: Labor Day, university holiday.
- September 11: For courses in session 001: Last day to register. Last day to receive a refund. Last day to enroll for a grade of Pass/No Pass or Audit. Last day to purchase or waive Tuition Refund Insurance.
- September 15: Last day to add/drop a Monday-only class without a mark of "W" and receive a refund, or change Monday-only class to Pass/No Pass or Audit for session 001.

## October
- October 8-9: Fall Recess.
- October 9: For courses in session 001: Last day to change Pass/No Pass to a Letter Grade. Last day to drop a course without a mark of "W" on the transcript.

## November
- November 11: Veterans Day, non-instructional day.
- November 13: Last day to withdraw a course for session 001.
- November 25-29: Thanksgiving holiday

## December
- December 4: Fall semester classes end.
- December 5 – 8: Study days.
- December 9 – 16: Final Exams.
- December 9-22: Final grade submission via Grading and Roster System (GRS) (the page says "for fall 2025, Session 001").

# Spring 2027

## October
- October 5: Registration appointment times and permit to register information will be available for review at my.usc.edu. Students may register through Web Registration.
- October 26: Registration for continuing students begins

## January
- January 4 – 8: Registration for spring semester continues
- January 8: Last day to register and settle without late fee for session 001
- January 11: Spring semester classes begin
- January 18: Martin Luther King Day, university holiday
- January 29: For courses in session 001: Last day to register for classes. Last day to drop a class and receive a refund for a course. Last day to enroll for a course for a grade of Pass/No Pass or Audit. Last day to purchase or waive tuition refund insurance.

## February
- February 2: Last day to add/drop a Monday-only class without a mark of "W" and receive a refund, or change the Monday-only class to Pass/No Pass or Audit for session 001
- February 15: President's Day, university holiday
- February 26: For courses in session 001: Last day to drop a course without a mark of "W" on the transcript for session 001. Last day to change a Pass/No Pass course to Letter Grade for session 001.

## March
- March 14 – 21: Spring recess

## April
- April 9: Last day to withdraw a course for session 001
- April 30: Spring semester classes end

## May
- May 1 – 4: Study days.
- May 5 – 12: Final examinations.
- May 5 – 18: Final Grade submission via Grading and Roster System (GRS)

## Limited Status Students

Limited status students may request spring 2027 enrollment starting November 19. The deadline for Limited Status registration is December 14.
