## Approval process

Before final approval can be granted from USC Trojan Event Services, you must complete this form, with the required approval signatures. Although authorization from Hospitality Services and the University Bookstore is required, it does not guarantee approval by USC Trojan Event Services.

This form serves as a fundraising approval request only. Events are not confirmed until you receive confirmation from USC Trojan Event Services. All of the following signatures need to be obtained in the order they are listed. First, please obtain a signature from Campus Activities followed by the Bookstore and/or Hospitality. Return the completed form to Trojan Event Services AND Campus Activities.

## Signatures (in order)

1. *Campus Activities Signature / Date / Comments
2. Bookstore Signature / Date / Comments
3. Hospitality Signature / Date / Comments

## Cancellation

The University of Southern California reserves the right to cancel any event, in whole or in part: If fees are not paid 2 weeks prior to the event, if the group does not comply with USC policies and procedures, or if USC officials cannot ensure the safety of students, faculty, staff or visitors.
